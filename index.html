Three taps, start to finish

The label does the typing for you

01

Snap the label

Point your camera and tap once. Front and back, at the winery or across the dinner table — it takes a second.

02

Let it read

Label recognition pulls the name, producer, vintage, and region automatically — and waits for you to correct anything it missed.

03

Make it yours

Add a rating, the price, a few words on the finish, and who you shared it with. Saved to your private cellar in seconds.

Photography-first

A wall of labels, not a spreadsheet

Your collection looks like the wines themselves — large, beautiful label photos you can scroll through like a photo album. The bottle you can't quite name is the one you can always picture.

  • ✓ Front & back labels, pinch to zoom for the fine print
  • ✓ Date, place, and occasion captured from the photo itself
  • ✓ Works offline — log a tasting room with no signal, sync later

Quietly thorough, never demanding

Everything a serious taster wants to remember, captured with the lightest possible touch.

Private by default

Every bottle is yours alone. No public profile, no follower count — share a single tasting note only if and when you choose to.

Find anything in seconds

Search by producer, varietal, region, rating, or price. "All the Pinot under $30" is one tap away when you're planning dinner.

Remembers the moment

The winery, the restaurant, the night in — location and date come straight from the photo, with the people you shared it with one tap away.

Notes in your own words

Aroma, flavor, finish — or just "perfect with the lamb." No rigid forms. Write as little or as much as the bottle deserves.
