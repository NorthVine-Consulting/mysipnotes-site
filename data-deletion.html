Account & Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your mysipnotes account and associated data

How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your mysipnotes account and all associated data by emailing us. We aim to process all deletion requests within 30 days.

  1. 1 Send an email to support@northvineconsulting.com from the email address associated with your mysipnotes account.
  2. 2 Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request — mysipnotes"
  3. 3 In the body of the email, confirm that you want your account and data permanently deleted. You may optionally include your display name to help us identify your account.
  4. 4 We will verify your identity by confirming the request came from the email address on file, then permanently delete your account and data within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when the deletion is complete.
Email Us to Delete Your Account

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following data from our systems:

Data type Action on deletion
Account credentials — email address, hashed password, or Google Sign-In link Permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication
Tasting notes & wine entries — wine names, ratings, tasting notes, and all other fields you entered Permanently deleted from Firebase Firestore
Photos — wine label photos you uploaded Permanently deleted from Firebase Cloud Storage
Voice notes / audio recordings — audio recordings attached to tasting entries Permanently deleted from Firebase Cloud Storage
Location data — coordinates and place names attached to tasting entries Permanently deleted along with the wine entries above
User profile — display name, profile photo, and app settings Permanently deleted from Firebase Firestore

What May Be Retained

A small amount of data may be retained after account deletion for legal, financial, or fraud-prevention purposes:

Data type Retention period & reason
Transaction records — records of subscription purchases processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store Retained for up to 7 years as required for tax and accounting compliance. These records are held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat; we do not retain your payment card details.
Anonymized purchase identifiers — RevenueCat may retain an anonymized, non-personally-identifiable identifier to prevent subscription fraud Retained by RevenueCat per their own data retention policy. This cannot be linked back to you once your account is deleted.

We do not sell your personal information, and no personally identifiable data is retained for advertising or marketing purposes after your account is deleted.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at support@northvineconsulting.com. You can also review our full Privacy Policy for more information about how we handle your data.
